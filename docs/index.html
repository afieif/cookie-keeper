See every cookie, even the hidden ones

Looking cookies up by the page’s address alone misses some of them. Cookie Keeper also shows:

  • Cookies set on other paths, like /products
  • Partitioned (CHIPS) cookies
  • Cookies from sites embedded in the page
  • Changes as they happen, without reopening

Delete all. Keep what matters.

Pin the cookies you care about, like a login, and “Delete all” skips them. It only ever deletes the cookies you can see, so narrow the list first if you like.

Protect a cookie. Block the rest.

  • Protect a cookie and any change or deletion by a site is undone straight away.
  • Block a cookie and it’s deleted the moment a site sets it, by name or for a whole domain.
  • Edit every field: name, value, domain, path, expiry, SameSite, Secure, HttpOnly and partition.

Imports that explain themselves

Paste or drop JSON from other cookie tools, a Playwright storageState file, a Netscape cookies.txt, or a raw Cookie: header. If a cookie can’t be added, you’re told exactly why, instead of seeing “Unknown error”.

Backups on a schedule

Take a snapshot whenever you like, or every hour, day or week. Restore one with a click or download it as JSON. Backups are stored only in your browser.

Side panel & DevTools

Keep it open beside the page as you switch tabs, or use the Cookies tab in DevTools. You can turn the DevTools tab off.

Every site at once

Switch between this site, this site plus its subdomains, or every cookie in the browser.

Incognito aware

Switch between your normal and incognito cookie stores. Incognito cookies are never backed up.

Quick copy

Copy any value with one click, or copy a whole cookie as JSON.

Light & dark

Follows your system theme. Every control has a text label, and nothing animates.

Safe exports

A clear warning reminds you that exported cookies are live logins, a common way people get tricked out of their accounts.

Nothing leaves your browser

Cookie Keeper has no servers and makes no network requests. Your cookies stay on your device, and the code is open so you can check for yourself.

Install

Cookie Keeper is on its way to the Chrome Web Store. Until then, you can install it from source in about a minute. It works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers.

  1. Download the code

    On GitHub, choose Code → Download ZIP and unzip it, or run git clone https://github.com/afieif/cookie-keeper.

  2. Open the extensions page

    Go to chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.

  3. Load it

    Click Load unpacked and choose the cookie-keeper folder. Then pin it to your toolbar.
